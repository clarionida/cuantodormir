We’re Clariónida, a group of curious, multipotentialite people who enjoy learning about everything and creating things with care. We made cuánto dormir so that deciding when to go to bed, wake up or take a nap feels a little easier. What you find here is based on published research, and on every page we tell you where it comes from. We hope it helps you rest better.

## Why cuánto dormir is different

- **Made by Clariónida, with scientific sources cited.** Every fact comes with its reference, and the formula is in plain view in [How we calculate](/en/how-we-calculate/).
- **Honest about what’s approximate.** The 90 minutes of a cycle are an average; we tell you so and let you adjust it.
- **No ads and no tracking.** We don’t use cookies or third-party trackers. Read our [Privacy](/en/privacy/) page.
- **Made for nighttime.** Soft, dark colors, clear type and nothing that wakes you up more than it should. It works offline after your first visit.
- **With a nap calculator.** Beyond the night, it helps you choose how long to sleep in the afternoon.
