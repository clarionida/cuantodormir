## What we don’t do

- We don’t use cookies.
- We don’t use third-party trackers or show ads.
- We don’t ask for your location, your name or your email.
- We don’t load fonts or any other resources from Google: everything comes from this same site.

## What’s saved on your device

So you don’t have to choose the same things every night, we save in your browser (with `localStorage`) how long it takes you to fall asleep, your cycle length if you adjusted it, the last times you chose and your preferred language. All of that stays on your device: it never reaches us or anyone else. You can delete it whenever you like from your browser settings, by clearing this site’s data.

When you calculate, your choices also appear in the page address (for example `/en/?despertar=08:00&latencia=15`). That way you can go back, reload or share the result. They’re only shared if you share the link.

## Offline use

The site saves a copy of the pages you visit on your device, so the calculator works even without internet. It’s a local copy that only your browser uses.

## Videos

In the meditations and sounds section, YouTube videos don’t load until you tap the play button. We use the version of YouTube that doesn’t store cookies (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play a video, YouTube receives the request just as it would on any site, and an ad may play before the video.

## Donations

If you decide to buy me a tea, the payment happens on Stripe’s page, which opens in a new tab and has its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.

## Analytics

Right now we don’t measure visits. If we ever do, it will be with a tool that doesn’t use cookies or follow you from site to site (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or GoatCounter), and we’ll say so here.
