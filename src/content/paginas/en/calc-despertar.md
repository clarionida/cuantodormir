## What time should you go to bed to wake up on time?

To wake up feeling lighter, it helps if your alarm goes off at the end of a sleep cycle rather than in the middle of one. A cycle lasts about 90 minutes on average, so we count back from your wake-up time in 90-minute steps and subtract the time it takes you to fall asleep.

The formula is simple:

<p class="formula">bedtime = wake-up time − n × 90 min − time to fall asleep</p>

For example, if you want to wake up at 8:00 AM and it takes you about 15 minutes to fall asleep, with 6 cycles (9 hours of sleep) you’d close your eyes at 10:45 PM, and with 5 cycles (7 h 30 min), at 12:15 AM. We suggest 5 or 6 cycles because guidelines for adults recommend sleeping 7 hours or more per night on a regular basis ([Watson et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25979105/)).

The 90 minutes are an average: in a study of 6,064 cycles recorded in a sleep lab, the median was 96 minutes, with a lot of variation from person to person ([Sleep Health, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37914631/)). That’s why this calculator is a gentle approximation, not an exact rule. If you often wake up groggy, you can try “Adjust my cycle.”
