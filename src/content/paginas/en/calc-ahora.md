## If I fall asleep now, when should I wake up?

If you’re going to sleep right now, the best times to wake up are the ones that land at the end of a sleep cycle. We take the time on your phone, add how long it takes you to fall asleep and then add cycles of about 90 minutes.

<p class="formula">wake-up time = now + time to fall asleep + n × 90 min</p>

For example, if it’s 11:00 PM and it takes you about 15 minutes to fall asleep, your options are 12:45 AM, 2:15 AM, 3:45 AM, 5:15 AM, 6:45 AM and 8:15 AM. The last two (5 and 6 cycles, from 7 h 30 min to 9 h) are the ones we suggest, in line with the 7 hours or more that guidelines recommend for adults ([Watson et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25979105/)).

We use your device’s time, in your time zone, without asking for your location. The calculations use real dates, so they work even if you cross midnight or the clocks change for daylight saving time. If more than 5 minutes go by, you can recalculate with the time at that moment.

Keep in mind that the 90 minutes are an average and everyone is a little different ([Sleep Health, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37914631/)). Read [how long it takes you to fall asleep](/guia/cuanto-tardas-en-dormirte/) (in Spanish) or [how this calculator works](/en/how-we-calculate/).
