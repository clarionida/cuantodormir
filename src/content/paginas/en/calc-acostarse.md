## What time should I wake up if I go to bed at a certain time?

If you already know when you’ll go to bed, you can plan your alarm so it goes off at the end of a sleep cycle. Choose the time you’ll close your eyes, and we add how long it takes you to fall asleep and then cycles of about 90 minutes.

<p class="formula">wake-up time = bedtime + time to fall asleep + n × 90 min</p>

For example, if you go to bed at 10:00 PM and it takes you about 15 minutes to fall asleep, your options are 11:45 PM, 1:15 AM, 2:45 AM, 4:15 AM, 5:45 AM and 7:15 AM. We suggest the 5- and 6-cycle options (5:45 AM and 7:15 AM), because guidelines recommend that adults sleep 7 hours or more on a regular basis ([Watson et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25979105/)).

This tab is handy for planning your week: if you go to bed at similar times, you also wake up at similar times. The 90 minutes are an average, with variation from person to person ([Sleep Health, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37914631/)), so think of it as a guide.
